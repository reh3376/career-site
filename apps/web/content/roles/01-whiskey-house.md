Executive team member, with ownership of P&L, budget and the software
development lifecycle. Led the electrical, process, controls, software
and data-engineering teams.

Built the greenfield Whiskey House distillery: a 24/7 production plant
running on the MES, WMS, historian, OT/SCADA and governance software
created in-house rather than bought.

Owned electrical safety for the site, directing third-party
short-circuit, coordination and arc-flash studies and leading
implementation of the findings under NFPA 70E.

The build drew national coverage for being unusually technology-led,
in Wired and Forbes and in a systems integrator's case study.
