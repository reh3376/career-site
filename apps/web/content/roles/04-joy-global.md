Senior Electrical Engineer for control systems at Joy Global, reporting
to the area director, in a manufacturing business where equipment is
designed, built on a production floor, tested and then commissioned in
the field. MSHA governed the work throughout, so a design error was a
safety event rather than a defect report.

Started in the surface mining division on draglines and shovels, then
moved to the underground division: conveyors, crushers, longwall
miners, continuous miners and shuttle cars. Designed, built and
maintained the control systems for that equipment.

Designed surface and underground power distribution from 12,470 V down
to 480 V three phase, including transformers, switchgear and motor
control centers, and engineered AC/DC inverter and converter systems
for underground equipment operating below 999 V.

Produced single-line and schematic designs in AutoCAD Electrical,
specified components, and issued bills of material and
design-to-manufacturing packages. Supported factory acceptance testing
and field commissioning to MSHA and National Electrical Code
requirements.

Led, trained and mentored the technicians who built, tested and
maintained the systems.

The longest single stay of the career, and the bridge between the
telecom infrastructure years and the process-control years that
followed.
