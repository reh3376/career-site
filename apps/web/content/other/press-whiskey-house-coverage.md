# Press: Whiskey House of Kentucky

Three pieces of coverage of the Whiskey House build. **These are
citations, not summaries.** None of the three could be read directly:
Wired blocks automated readers, Forbes returned 403, and the video
cannot be transcribed from here. What follows is the metadata that
could be verified, and nothing about what the pieces argue or what
they say about any individual.

That restraint is the point. A search engine's summary of the Forbes
piece described Roger as a "20-year industrial automation veteran" with
a background "in the oil and gas sector as well as the automotive
industry", none of which matches the record on this site: thirty years
across power and telecom infrastructure, mining equipment at Joy
Global, then distilled spirits. Writing a description from a snippet
would have imported that error. Anything below is either from the URL
itself or from a named secondary source.

Whiskey House, the $135M greenfield distillery where Roger was VP of
Engineering and Technology from 2022 to 2026, drew national coverage
