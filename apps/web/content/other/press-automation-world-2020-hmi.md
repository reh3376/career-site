# Press: Automation World, 20 March 2020

"Bardstown Bourbon Upgrades HMI for Strategic Connectivity", by David
Greenfield, Automation World.
<https://www.automationworld.com/control/article/21123207/bardstown-bourbon-upgrades-hmi-for-strategic-connectivity>

Trade-press coverage of the Ignition HMI and SCADA work at Bardstown
Bourbon Company, completed in autumn 2019 in under a week.

## What the article reports

The legacy HMI was replaced with Inductive Automation's Ignition
platform, chosen for data connectivity and flexibility rather than for
the screens alone. The article reports Bardstown Bourbon as the first
US distillery to implement Ignition in this way.

Named in the piece: ERP integration delivering work orders
automatically; a custom batch-tracking system following production from
mash cooker to cistern; the Perspective module giving supervisors
read-only data on mobile devices; and a DVR playback module from ECS
Solutions for reviewing historical data. Operators kept a familiar
interface through the change. A batch sequencing engine and
consolidated inventory control are described as planned next steps.

## Roger, as quoted

The article quotes him directly:

> "The ability to historize an unlimited number of data points has
> changed the way we do business and make decisions."

**It gives his title at the time as plant engineer.** The timeline on
this site records the Bardstown Bourbon role as Director of
Engineering, 2018 to 2022. Both can be true of different points in a
four-year tenure, and the published title is recorded here as
published rather than reconciled to the later one.
