Awarded to Bardstown Bourbon Company in 2021, for the natural-gas
conservation work and for engaging the Kentucky Pollution Prevention
Center on sustainable value-stream mapping to evaluate energy and water
use.

The award goes annually to an organisation that has "demonstrated a
commitment to the principles of sustainability and environmental
stewardship", presented during National Pollution Prevention Week. It
is the second recognition for the same programme, alongside the EPA
ENERGY STAR Project of the Year.

Source: the Kentucky Pollution Prevention Center's award page,
<https://centers.louisville.edu/pollutionprevention/about/environmental-sustainability-award>
